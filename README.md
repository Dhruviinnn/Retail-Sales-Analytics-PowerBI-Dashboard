# 📊 Retail Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

A professional multi-page Power BI dashboard built using the Sample Superstore dataset. This project provides interactive business insights into sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and regional performance.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard was developed to help business stakeholders monitor key performance indicators, identify trends, and support data-driven decision making.

The report consists of five interactive pages:

- 📈 Executive Dashboard
- 💰 Sales Analytics
- 👥 Customer Analytics
- 📦 Product Analytics
- 🌍 Regional & Shipping Analytics

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Microsoft Excel

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ Executive Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of business performance.

**KPIs**

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value

**Visuals**

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Top Products
- Segment Distribution

---

### 2️⃣ Sales Analytics

Focuses on sales trends and performance.

**KPIs**

- Total Sales
- Sales Growth %
- Average Order Value
- Total Orders

**Visuals**

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Yearly Sales Trend
- Sales by Segment
- Sales by Ship Mode
- Sales Forecast

---

### 3️⃣ Customer Analytics

Analyzes customer purchasing behavior.

**KPIs**

- Total Customers
- Average Sales per Customer
- Average Profit per Customer
- Average Order Value

**Visuals**

- Sales vs Profit by Segment
- Customer Distribution by Region
- Top Customers
- Sales by Segment
- Customer Orders Map

---

### 4️⃣ Product Analytics

Evaluates product performance.

**KPIs**

- Total Products
- Average Sales per Product
- Best Selling Category
- Average Discount

**Visuals**

- Top Selling Products
- Loss-Making Products
- Profit by Sub-Category
- Sales by Category
- Discount vs Profit

---

### 5️⃣ Regional & Shipping Analytics

Provides geographical and logistics insights.

**KPIs**

- Top Sales Region
- Top Sales State
- Most Used Ship Mode
- Average Sales per Shipment

**Visuals**

- Sales by Region
- Profit by Region
- Top States by Sales
- Ship Mode Analysis
- State Performance Map

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- West region generated the highest sales.
- Technology is the best-selling category.
- Consumer segment contributed the highest revenue.
- Standard Class is the most frequently used shipping mode.
- Several products generated negative profit despite high sales.
- Product discounts have a negative relationship with profitability.
